Executive summary
Method

Benefits. Labor savings = volume × share automated × (1 − residual human time) × minutes ÷ 60 × loaded rate. Error savings = automated volume × error rate × reduction × cost per error. Benefits ramp linearly to full run-rate.

Costs. One-time cost lands at go-live (month 0). Run cost is spread evenly by month.

Returns. NPV discounts monthly cash flows at the annual rate converted to monthly. IRR is solved numerically and annualized. Payback is the month cumulative cash turns positive.

Capacity. Hours freed ÷ 1,800 productive hours per FTE-year. Freed capacity is usually redeployed rather than cut — the summary says so.
